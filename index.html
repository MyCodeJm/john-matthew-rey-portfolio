Excel Project

Personal Expense Dashboard

A personal finance dashboard built in Excel, designed so anyone can read it in a few seconds. Log an income or expense once and every figure, chart, and the plain-language summary at the top update on their own — the sheet always reports your three most recent months. Built to show real Excel proficiency: structured tables, cross-sheet formulas, conditional formatting, and charts rather than typed-in numbers. All figures in Philippine pesos.

How it's built

  • Three linked sheets — an Income log, an Expense log, and a Budget sheet. Both logs use dropdowns for Category and Payment Method so entries stay consistent.
  • Rolling 3-month window — the sheet finds the newest date in your data and automatically reports only the three most recent months. Log an April salary and it drops January and picks up April on its own.
  • Formula-driven, not typed in — every figure comes from SUMIFS, INDEX/MATCH, and EOMONTH, so nothing needs editing when new rows are added.
  • Written in plain language — a line at the top says which category cost the most and what share of your income you kept, generated by formula rather than written by hand.
  • Colour does the explaining — money left over shows green, overspending shows red, and data bars in the "% of spending" column make the biggest costs obvious at a glance.

What it tracks

  • Four headline numbers: money in, money out, how much you saved, and whether you came in under budget
  • Spending across six categories, with how much budget is left in each
  • Month-by-month money in, money out, and savings
  • Three charts: income vs. spending, category vs. budget, and savings per month
